About WildRoots Zoo

WildRoots Zoo is a wildlife information hub and conservation-oriented sanctuary welcoming families, students, and nature lovers.

Our mission is wildlife conservation

We create immersive habitats and support rescue programs while teaching visitors about animal welfare, biodiversity, and sustainable habitats.

  • Natural habitats designed for animal comfort and enrichment.
  • Education programs for schools and families.
  • Rescue efforts for endangered species.
  • Community outreach for conservation awareness.

Why Zoos Matter

Modern zoos are critical for conservation, research, and public awareness about wildlife challenges.

Conservation

Protect endangered animals

Zoos care for species with critical population declines and offer breeding programs to restore numbers safely.

Education

Inspire future conservationists

Visitors learn about animal biology, habitats, and climate impacts through engaging exhibits and live talks.

Research

Support scientific discovery

Animal care teams observe behavior, nutrition, and health to improve survival strategies in wild and captive settings.

Habitat care

Preserve natural ecosystems

Zoos promote habitat protection initiatives and partner with conservation groups worldwide.

Featured Animals

Meet our key residents across habitats, from majestic mammals to vibrant marine life.

African Lion

Panthera leo Habitat: Savanna

Diet: Carnivore

Lifespan: 10–14 years

Status: Vulnerable

Fact: Lions live in social groups called prides and are the only cats that do so.

Scarlet Macaw

Ara macao Habitat: Rainforest

Diet: Fruits & seeds

Lifespan: 40–50 years

Status: Least Concern

Fact: Macaws use their powerful beaks to crack nuts and climb tree branches.

Asian Elephant

Elephas maximus Habitat: Forest

Diet: Herbivore

Lifespan: 60–70 years

Status: Endangered

Fact: Elephants communicate using low-frequency sounds humans often cannot hear.

Sea Turtle

Chelonia mydas Habitat: Ocean

Diet: Seagrass

Lifespan: 60–80 years

Status: Endangered

Fact: Sea turtles return to the exact beach where they were born to lay eggs.

Masai Giraffe

Giraffa camelopardalis Habitat: Grassland

Diet: Leaves

Lifespan: 25 years

Status: Vulnerable

Fact: A giraffe's neck contains the same number of vertebrae as a human neck.

Jaguar

Panthera onca Habitat: Tropical forest

Diet: Carnivore

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Status: Near Threatened

Fact: Jaguars are strong swimmers and often hunt in rivers and lakes.

Poison Dart Frog

Dendrobatidae Habitat: Rainforest

Diet: Insects

Lifespan: 10–15 years

Status: Varies by species

Fact: Their bright colors warn predators of their toxic skin secretions.

Greater Flamingo

Phoenicopterus roseus Habitat: Wetlands

Diet: Brine shrimp

Lifespan: 20–30 years

Status: Least Concern

Fact: Flamingos get their pink color from pigments in their food.

Bottlenose Dolphin

Tursiops truncatus Habitat: Aquarium

Diet: Fish

Lifespan: 40–50 years

Status: Least Concern

Fact: Dolphins are highly intelligent and use echolocation to navigate underwater.

White Rhino

Ceratotherium simum Habitat: Grassland

Diet: Herbivore

Lifespan: 35–45 years

Status: Near Threatened

Fact: Rhinos have thick skin that acts like armor and remarkable hearing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common visitor questions answered clearly and quickly.

What are the zoo opening hours?

The zoo is open every day from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM with last entry at 6:15 PM.

Can I buy tickets online?

Yes, tickets can be purchased online for faster entry and discounted family passes.

Is the zoo wheelchair accessible?

Yes, the zoo offers wheelchair-accessible paths, parking, and restrooms.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are not permitted, except for certified service animals.

Is photography allowed?

Personal photography is welcome. Please avoid flash and respect animal comfort.

Can I bring outside food?

Outside food is permitted only in designated picnic areas. Do not feed animals.

Do you offer guided tours?

Guided tours are available on weekends and during school holidays. Book in advance.

What safety measures do you follow?

We monitor animal welfare, maintain clean barriers, and provide first aid support on site.

Are school visits available?

Yes, educational programs and group discounts are available for school visits.

How can I support conservation?

Support our zoo through donations, membership, volunteering, and spreading awareness.
