Oat Meal

RECEPIE FOR A OAT MEAL

oatmeal is highly nutritious and offers multiple health benefits, including heart health, blood sugar control, weight management, and digestive support.

Ingridients

  • Oats
  • Milk
  • Dry-fruits
  • Honey

Instructions

  1. Boil the (100ml) water in a bowl on stove.
  2. Add(100gm)oats and (200ml) of milk
  3. turn off the stove and add honey mix well.
